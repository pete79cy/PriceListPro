### ✅ UI Modernization Suggestions:

1. **Compact Card Design**:

   * Reduce padding/margins.
   * Use icons with labels inline (side-by-side) instead of stacked.

2. **Typography & Spacing**:

   * Use a cleaner, modern font (like Inter or Roboto).
   * Reduce font size slightly (e.g., 14px instead of 16px).
   * Add tighter line-height.

3. **Visual Hierarchy**:

   * Make timestamps lighter (gray) and smaller.
   * Use subtle divider lines or background shading to segment events.

4. **Color & Icons**:

   * Use flat design with subtle hover effects.
   * Keep icons minimal and consistent in style.
