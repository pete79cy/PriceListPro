How Quotations Work
Creating a Quotation:
  1. Upload an Excel file or PDF containing the products your customer has requested
  2. The system will parse the file and extract product information
  3. Products will be matched with your product catalog when possible
  4. For each product, the system will check if it exists in the customer's price list
  5. If found, pricing information will be auto-populated
  6. You'll be able to review and edit all details before finalizing
Editing and Finalizing:
  1. After upload, you'll be presented with an editable interface
  2. You can adjust selling prices, VAT rates, suppliers, and cost prices
  3. For each product, you can specify if it's sourced from a supplier or produced in-house
  4. Once finalized, you can generate a PDF quotation to send to your customer
  5. You can also create supplier-specific order reports by grouping products by supplier
