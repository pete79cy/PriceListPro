Plant Pricing System Help

Getting Started

The Plant Pricing System helps you manage customer-specific plant price lists and process invoices.

Key Features
  • Upload and process Excel price lists with customer-specific pricing
  • Parse PDF invoices to extract line items and prices
  • Create and manage customer quotations with supplier reports
  • Manage products with scientific names, categories, and pot sizes
  • Track customer-specific pricing
  • Search across products and pricing data
  • AI-powered document insights for quotations and invoices
Using the System
  1. Customers: Add and manage your customer database
  2. Products: Maintain your product catalog with details like scientific names and pot sizes
  3. Upload Files:
    • Upload Excel files for price lists
    • Upload PDF invoices for automatic processing
    • Download template for correct Excel format
  4. Price Lists: View and manage customer-specific pricing
  5. Invoices: Access processed invoices and their line items
  6. Quotations: Create, manage and export customer quotations and supplier reports
  7. Suppliers: Manage supplier information for quotations and product sourcing
    • Maintain supplier contact information
    • Track supplier products with the Supplier Products tool
    • Generate supplier-specific catalogs and reports
  8. Price Updates: Review and approve pending price changes
  9. Search: Quick access to pricing information
  10. AI Document Insights: Get intelligent analysis of your documents, quotations, and invoices
  11. API: Access the system programmatically through REST API endpoints for quotation management
File Formats

Excel Price Lists:

  • Required columns: Name, Price
  • Optional columns: Category, Scientific Name, Actual Size
  • Download the template for correct formatting

PDF Invoices:

  • System extracts: Invoice number, date, line items
  • Automatically matches products and updates price lists
  • Creates price update requests for significant changes
