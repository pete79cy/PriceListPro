What is an Invoice Addendum?

An invoice addendum is a supplementary document that lists detailed sales transactions for a specific customer during a billing period. It can be attached to the main invoice to provide additional breakdown of sales.

Required Information:
  • Customer who will receive the addendum
  • Invoice number it will be attached to
  • Date range for the sales period
Next Steps:

After creating the addendum, you'll be able to add individual sale line items with products, quantities, prices, and VAT rates.
